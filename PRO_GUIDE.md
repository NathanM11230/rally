# Rally Pro Guide

Rally is the club's lesson and reminder app. Use it to track your assigned
lessons, clinics, junior groups, events, contacts, reminders, calendar adds, and
pay-period records.

## Getting Started

1. Open Rally and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your approved email or club invite code, plus your name and phone
   number.
3. Open the Supabase invitation sent to your email and choose your Rally
   password.
4. Return to Rally and log in.

Your phone number is saved to your profile. Rally uses it when you are assigned
to lessons, but lesson reminders are still sent manually from your own phone.

## Dashboard

The Dashboard shows your upcoming assigned lessons.

Use it to:

- See today's lessons.
- See upcoming lessons.
- Open reminder text links.
- Mark reminders as sent.
- Add lessons to your personal calendar.
- Edit lesson details.

Only lessons assigned to you show on your Dashboard.

## Creating A Lesson

Click **New lesson**.

Choose the lesson type:

- Lesson
- Clinic
- Other event
- Freshmen
- Varsity
- Team

Then select:

- Assigned pro or pros
- Student or participant names and phone numbers
- Lesson date
- Lesson time
- Court or courts
- Optional notes

Lesson times are available every 15 minutes from 6:00 AM through 9:00 PM.

For courts, open the court picker and select one or more courts. Rally supports
Tennis Courts 1-8, Pickleball Courts 1-4, and Paddle Courts 1-5.

## Students And Contacts

When you save a lesson with a name and phone number, Rally saves that person as
a contact.

When creating a future lesson, start typing the person's name. Rally will show
matching saved contacts. Select the correct contact and Rally fills the name and
phone number automatically.

You can also go to **Contacts** and click **New contact** to save someone
without creating a lesson first.

## Sending Reminder Texts

Rally does not send texts automatically.

Instead, Rally creates prefilled text messages for you. Tap the reminder text
button, review the message in your phone's texting app, and send it yourself.

After sending, return to Rally and mark the reminder as sent. This helps avoid
duplicate reminders.

Rally will not show you a reminder text link to text yourself.

## Calendar

Each lesson has an **Add to calendar** link.

Use this to add the lesson to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, or most
phone calendar apps. The calendar event includes the lesson type, participants,
pros, courts, notes, and a default one-hour duration.

## Editing Or Deleting A Lesson

Open a lesson and click **Edit**.

Any assigned pro can edit the lesson. You can update:

- Lesson type
- Assigned pros
- Participants
- Date and time
- Courts
- Notes
- Status

You can also delete a lesson if it should no longer be tracked.

## Pay Periods

Use **Pay Periods** to check your assigned lessons for each two-week pay period.

The page shows:

- Total items in the period
- Counts by lessons, clinics, junior groups, teams, and other events
- A detailed list grouped by type
- A print option

This is meant to help you compare what you taught or had scheduled against what
you were paid for.

## Follow Ups

Use **Follow ups** to see people you work with often.

Rally creates quick text links like:

```txt
Hey [FirstName], any time to hit soon?
```

The message is not sent automatically. It opens in your texting app for you to
review and send.

## Quick Tips

- Keep your profile phone number up to date.
- Use saved contacts instead of retyping phone numbers.
- Mark reminders as sent after you text.
- Add lessons to your calendar if you want them on your personal phone calendar.
- Check Pay Periods before or after payroll to catch missing lessons.
